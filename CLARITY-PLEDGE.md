# The Clarity Pledge

Anyone who forks, remixes, or contributes to UniversalOS agrees to these five lines.

1. **I'll say what I'm building before I build it.** The promise is written down, in plain words.
2. **I'll show the receipts.** Promise, build, proof, all public.
3. **I'll mark what's unfinished.** Gaps are labeled, not hidden behind dashboards or slogans.
4. **I'll keep it forkable.** Anyone can take what I made without asking permission.
5. **I'll serve people, not performances.** No puppets, no mascots, no cosplay.

If my build doesn't match my promise, it isn't done, and I'll say so.

*Out of many, one. For real this time.*
