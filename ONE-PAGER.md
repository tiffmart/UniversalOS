# UniversalOS — one-pager

**The civic OS for people done with cosplay systems.**

Open-source clarity. No slogans. No puppets. Just receipts.

---

## The problem
Most systems sell a promise and ship a costume.
Slogans, mascots, "new normals" — and no receipts when the build doesn't match the speech.

People can feel it. They just don't have a clean frame to build against.

## What UniversalOS is
A human-first, AI-aligned **operating system for civic life** —
not as finished software, as a public blueprint anyone can fork, remix, and pressure-test.

It's the stake that says: **if it doesn't match the promise, it isn't done.**

## What it is not
- Not a phone / computer OS
- Not a political party
- Not a brand campaign
- Not therapy, cosplay, or vibes without structure

## Who it's for
Builders and remixers who want structure that stays honest under pressure:
- civic tech people
- organizers
- anyone designing systems that have to show their work

If you want a vibe, bounce.  
If you want a forkable frame with teeth, stay.

## How you use it
1. **Read** the README, Clarity Pledge, and Roadmap
2. **Fork** what you need
3. **Fill** one real piece (a local rule, a transparency stub, a receipts template)
4. **Show work** — promise → build → proof

Small loop. Public. Incomplete on purpose — so the next person can actually ship something.

## The pitch (one breath)
We're done performing clarity.  
UniversalOS is the open blueprint for systems that match their promise — for real this time.
